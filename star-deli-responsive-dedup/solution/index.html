Star Deli

Authentic Polish Bakery

Baked Fresh Every Morning

Traditional recipes from Kraków, made by hand in Brooklyn.

Sernik

Polish cheesecake made with twaróg (farmer's cheese) for a denser, grainier texture. Flavored with vanilla and lemon zest, topped with glazed fruit.

Pączki

Deep-fried doughnuts filled with rose hip jam or custard, glazed with icing and topped with candied orange peel. A Fat Thursday favorite.

Makowiec

A sweet rolled pastry with dense poppy seed filling mixed with honey, nuts, and raisins. A staple of Christmas and Easter tables.
